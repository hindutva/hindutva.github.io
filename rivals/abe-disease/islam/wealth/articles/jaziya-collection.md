> The amount of the *jaziya*, or tax upon infidels, differed according to the wealth of the individual Parsi, but it was never less than two *tomans*. A *toman* is now worth about three shillings and eight pence, but it used to be worth much more. Even now, when money has much depreciated, it represents a labourer’s wage for ten days. The money had to be paid on the spot, when the *farrāsh*, who was acting as collector, met the man. The *farrāsh* was at liberty to do what he liked when collecting *jaziya*. The man was not even allowed to go home to fetch the money, but was at once beaten until it was given. **About 1865 a *farrash* collecting this tax tied a man to a dog, and gave a blow to each in turn.**
