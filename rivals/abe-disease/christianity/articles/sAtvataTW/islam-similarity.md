## Eternal punishment
3) Eternal punishment/damnation for nonbelievers is a central component of the traditional theology of both Christianity and Islam.
This has been a powerful psychological and theological motivator for religious expansion throughout history (sometimes via nonviolent means, sometimes via violence).

By contrast, Jewish views on the fate of non-Jews in the "world to come" vary considerably.

## virginity of Mary
4) Muslims traditionally believe in the perpetual virginity of Mary, which is one notable aspect where Islamic belief aligns more closely with Catholic and Orthodox doctrine than the Protestant Christian views regarding Mary.
