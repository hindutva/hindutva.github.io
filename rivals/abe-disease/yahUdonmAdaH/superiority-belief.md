## Daily statement
Traditional Orthodox Jewish men recite these three morning blessings (part of Birkot Hashachar):

> Shelo asani goy (Who has not made me a gentile)  
Shelo asani eved (Who has not made me a slave)  
Shelo asani ishah (Who has not made me a woman)

Women say a different version.

## Reward difference
The traditional reason is gratitude for the greater number of mitzvot a free Jewish man must fulfill (613) versus the seven Noahide laws for gentiles.  

Righteous Gentiles who keep the Noahide laws earn a share in the World to Come (Rambam, Kings 8:11).  
But Talmud (Kiddushin 31a) teaches that one commanded who fulfills gets greater reward than one not commanded who does the same. More mitzvot mean greater opportunity for closeness and reward.

This means that, in a sense, Gentiles are destined for less fruits in the afterlife (ie less divinity).
